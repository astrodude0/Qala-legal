Delete your account

infoThis is an English translation for reference. The Arabic version is binding.

This page explains how to delete your account in the Qala Store («متجر القلعة») customer app or the vendor app, and what happens to your data.

In the app (fastest)

  • Customer app: open "My account" → "Delete account", then type «حذف» to confirm.
  • Vendor app: open the Store tab → "Delete account", then type «حذف» to confirm. Your store will close permanently.

Without the app

Message us from the email address linked to your account at ma6640788@gmail.com, or on WhatsApp at +964 783 026 7250, and say which app (customer or vendor). We will confirm that the account is yours, delete it within 7 days and let you know.

What is deleted and what remains

  • Deleted immediately: your name and email, your saved addresses, your devices' notification tokens and your sign-in account. Your current orders are cancelled.
  • Customers: we erase your name, phone number, address and notes from your past orders; the order record (products, amounts and dates) remains without them.
  • Vendors: the store closes permanently and disappears from the customer app; its name, phone number, photo and products remain in the record of past orders.
  • Backups: deleted data disappears from them within 30 days.
  • Crash reports: deleted automatically within 90 days.

Details are in the privacy policies: customer app · vendor app.

If you signed in with Facebook

You can also remove Qala Store in your Facebook settings → "Apps and websites". That stops Facebook sharing your data with us, but it does not delete your account with us, so use one of the methods above as well.
